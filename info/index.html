it's cable two

cable two is a live public tv network making television for the digital age. cable two is a threat and a promise.

we stream tv shows, experiments, half-formed ideas, fake commercials, call-in chaos, and live moments that can never be repeated. it’s part tv channel, part art collective, part ongoing experiment in what livestreaming can be when you imagine greater.

we're a creative community that is constantly working, learning, and growing together. our goal is to provide the support, resources, and platform for artists to make what they want to make, and share it with the world.

anyone can be part of cable two, all you have to do is say hi.

why streaming????;?

when you’re watching a livestream, you’re there to see something that has never happened, and will never happen again. you’re not watching a finished thing. you get to be part of the happening. livestreaming and television both turn time into a shared space that people enter together, even if they’re far apart. streaming got pulled toward gaming because video games already have the structure built in. it’s easy to watch and easy to share. but the form is bigger than what it’s been used for. Cable Two exists to see what happens when livestreaming stops trying to be one thing and starts becoming whatever people can make together in real time.

how can i join cable two?


if you've got an idea for a show or project, send us a pitch. it doesn’t need to be polished or fully formed. it just needs to feel like something that could exist. write down what it is, what it might look like on stream, what you’d need to make it happen, and how often it would show up if it became real. once your pitch is ready, submit it to our pitch page for review. you gotta read all of the documents and tips beforehand. if your pitch gets accepted, you’ll move on to production.
feel free to join our discord or email us for advice. if you’re unsure, or just want to talk it through, chat with us any time. good luck!

history

cable two started as a way for friends to have fun together.
in early 2020, while everyone was learning how to bake bread and playing animal crossing, the founders of cable two discovered a joke forum called twitter2, which was promptly banned after 100,000 posts in two weeks for posting images of women smoking. this led to the creation of another forum, a spiritual successor to twitter2, where we could continue to hang out and joke around, soon forming a distinctive group voice.

we thought it would be cool to livestream together on a single account, imitating the format of classic television, because it was funny. we liked the notion that in this digital age, we can. this led to the creation of orbtelevision, a shared Twitch channel that served as a platform for experimental livestreaming. after several months we decided to rebrand the channel as cable two.

since its inception, cable two has tried to push the boundaries of what is possible in the world of streaming, but more than anything else, cable two is still friends making television together. we like everyone in our group, what we’re making, and we’d like to keep doing it. now, we’d like to extend that to others.
